How analytics works

Board creation counts use saved dates and remain after deletion. Team drafts and published copies count once. Older deleted boards may be missing.

Current inventory includes saved personal and team boards, including drafts and private boards. Talking cards are placements, so one avatar on three boards counts as three talking cards.

Minutes use verified voice-call seconds. Text time is kept separate. Conversations start with a visitor message, and pending voice sessions are counted after verification.

Activity totals are retained separately from transcripts. Transcripts keep their existing permissions and retention period.
