How Picamo analytics works

Private to platform administrators. Opening Picamo or receiving a greeting does not count. A text session starts with a user message and ends after 30 minutes without activity. Panel reopening continues the same session. Voice calls are counted only after provider verification; recently ended calls can take several minutes to appear.

Ordinary users, board owners, and team members count. Admin testing is excluded unless selected. Automated QA and other avatars are excluded.

Accounts and guest browsers are distinct identities, not a count of people. They are deduplicated across the selected period. Conversation counts follow the session start date; messages and voice time follow the date they occurred. A session can therefore contribute messages on a date when it did not start.

Historical Picamo activity cannot be reliably reconstructed and is not imported. Only metadata is retained here; this page does not store conversation transcripts.
